A gift made for one person only stands out. A birth chart belongs in this category. It is based on the moment and place at which the recipient was born, so it cannot be swapped for anyone else's. Even so, it does not suit every person or every occasion. This article helps you judge whether a birth chart is the right gift. It also shows what you need in order to order one.

## What a birth chart is

A birth chart, also called a natal chart or radix, is a snapshot of the sky for the moment and place of a birth. It shows which zodiac signs the Sun, Moon and planets were in. The zodiac signs are the twelve sections of 30 degrees each into which the sky is divided along the apparent path of the Sun, from Aries to Pisces.

The chart also includes the houses and the ascendant. The ascendant, also called the rising sign, is the zodiac sign that was rising on the eastern horizon at the place of birth when the person was born. Houses are twelve sections of the birth chart, each standing for an area of life such as relationships, work or home.

Astrology is a system of interpretation. A birth chart describes themes, tendencies and tensions in a symbolic language. It does not predict events and it is not a proven science. That is precisely what makes it interesting as a gift: it invites reflection without fixing anything in place.

## Which occasions it suits

### Birthday

The obvious occasion. The horoscope relates to the day of birth, so it fits a birthday particularly well. A milestone birthday in particular can be a good moment to look back at the path so far.

### Christmas

At Christmas many people look for a gift that is more personal than a voucher. A horoscope is not tied to a date and can be read at leisure once the holidays quieten down.

### Christening or the birth of a child

For a newborn, a birth chart is a keepsake of the moment they arrived in the world. The parents usually know the time of birth exactly, because it has only just been noted. The horoscope can be printed and kept, perhaps until the child is old enough to read it. Here it is important to see it as a description of tendencies, not as a verdict on who the child will become.

### No particular occasion

Some people give a horoscope simply as a sign that they have taken an interest in the other person. This works especially well if you have talked about astrology together before.

## Who it suits

**People who enjoy self-reflection.** Anyone who likes thinking about themselves, keeps a journal or enjoys conversations about personality will find plenty in a horoscope. The interpretation offers a language for thinking about their own strengths and contradictions.

**People who already like astrology.** Someone who knows their Sun sign and may have heard of the ascendant is often pleased to receive a detailed account of their own birth chart.

**Sceptics who value care.** Not everyone who views astrology critically will turn down a horoscope. Some appreciate a gift that is precisely calculated, calmly written and carefully designed. The calculation of the planetary positions is astronomy and can be checked. The interpretation remains an interpretation, which can be read with some distance.

### When it is less suitable

If the recipient rejects astrology outright or finds it irritating, a different gift is the better choice. You should also think carefully during times when someone is dealing with difficult questions. A horoscope gives no answers to questions about health or fate, and it should not be understood that way.

## What you need to order it

A birth chart requires these details:

- **First name** of the recipient
- **Date of birth**
- **Time of birth**, as precise as possible
- **Place of birth**

The time of birth matters because the ascendant changes about every two hours on average. Just a few minutes can decide the ascendant and the starting points of the houses, the house cusps, if they lie close to the boundary between two signs. The time is entered as it was locally at the time. The conversion to Universal Time, including summer time, is done using the official time zone database. You do not need to convert anything.

### Finding out the birth time discreetly

If the gift is meant to stay a surprise, the time is often the hardest part. A few ways to go about it:

- **Ask the parents.** They often still know the time and are usually happy to keep the gift to themselves.
- **Ask siblings or a partner.** Sometimes someone close knows the time or can check without drawing attention.
- **Documents.** The time of birth is usually recorded by the registry office of the place of birth. In Germany this is the Standesamt, which keeps the birth register. If it is not on the birth certificate, a full extract from the register can be requested there. Hospital records and family records often contain it too. These documents, however, are usually held by the recipient or their family.

### If the birth time is missing

If the time cannot be established, you can still order the horoscope, at the same price. The Sun sign, the planets' signs and most aspects remain certain. Aspects are particular angles between two planets which, in interpretation, show how their themes work together. The ascendant, the Midheaven and the houses, on the other hand, are uncertain. In that case, the chapters that rely on them are worded more cautiously.
