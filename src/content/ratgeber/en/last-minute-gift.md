It is Friday evening, the party starts tomorrow at three, and there is still no present. For exactly this situation, a birth chart works better than you might expect. Ordering takes a few minutes. A quarter of an hour later the finished PDF is in your inbox.

## How fast it really is

To order, you need four details about the person you want to surprise: first name, date of birth, time and place of birth. Add your own email address, because the horoscope comes to you, not to the person receiving it. That keeps the surprise safe. With the details at hand, you are done in two or three minutes.

Within 15 minutes the horoscope arrives as a PDF by email, at the weekend or late in the evening too. A delayed delivery or a parcel that only turns up the day after tomorrow is simply not an issue.

If you do not know the time of birth, you can still order, at the same price. Without the time, the ascendant and houses cannot be calculated, and the chapters that build on them are worded more cautiously.

## Why it still feels so personal

Last-minute presents have a certain reputation. Chocolates from the petrol station, a voucher, the book that is on every table right now. You can tell they had to happen fast.

A birth chart avoids that, because it is built from data that belongs to exactly one person. The place and minute of birth determine where the sun, moon and planets stood in the sky. A birthplace a hundred kilometres away or half an hour's difference can already mean a different ascendant, and with it a different picture.

Of the 34 pages, more than 20 are calculated and written for this one natal chart. The chapters range from personality and strengths to love, family and career. At the end there is an outlook on the periods that matter in the coming months. The remaining pages explain how to read a natal chart and tell the history of astrology in brief, so even someone who has never held a horoscope finds their way around.

## Turning the PDF into a lovely present

The quickest way is to forward the email or send the file by messenger, ideally with a few lines of your own.

Printed, it feels far more special. The light edition is made for this: warm paper white with dark type, economical to print and, on good paper, almost like a small book. The cover shows the recipient's natal chart and is meant for framing. In a simple frame it hangs on the wall long after the party is over.

The dark edition in deep night blue looks its best on screen, for example on a tablet. If you cannot decide, take both editions for €5 more, so €40 instead of €35.

## What to sort out beforehand

The time of birth is the trickiest part if the present is meant to stay a surprise. Parents or siblings often know it, and sometimes it is noted in family documents. The reliable source is the birth register at the registry office of the place of birth, but a request there takes longer than a quarter of an hour. So in an emergency: better to order without the time than not at all.

One last thought from our side. A horoscope is a text for reflecting on yourself, not a prediction. Someone who has no interest in astrology at all will probably be happier with something else. For everyone else, it is one of the few presents you can get at the last minute without it showing.
