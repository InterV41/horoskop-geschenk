Many people know their date and place of birth but not the exact time. Their parents remember only roughly, the certificate gives no time, or there is no one left to ask. The obvious question is whether a horoscope is worth having at all. The short answer is yes. A large part of the birth chart depends only on the date. A few points, however, cannot be determined without the time. This article shows where the line lies.

## What a birth chart actually calculates

A birth chart, also called a natal chart or radix, is a snapshot of the sky for the moment and place of your birth. It shows which zodiac signs the Sun, Moon and planets were in. The zodiac signs are the twelve equal sections of 30 degrees each into which the sky is divided along the apparent path of the Sun, from Aries to Pisces.

There are also points that arise from the rotation of the Earth: the ascendant, the Midheaven and the houses. These points depend heavily on the time of day. The positions of the planets, by contrast, change only a little over the course of a day.

Astrology is a system of interpretation. It describes themes in a symbolic language, it makes no predictions and it is not a proven science. The calculation of the positions themselves, though, is pure astronomy and can be checked precisely.

## What stays certain without a birth time

### The Sun sign

The Sun moves about one degree a day across the sky. Your Sun sign is therefore fixed by the date. The time only matters if the Sun moved from one sign into the next on that very day. Even then, the moment of the change can be calculated, and a rough indication such as "in the morning" or "in the evening" is often enough.

### The planets' signs

Mercury, Venus and Mars cover at most around two degrees a day, and the outer planets much less. Their sign is therefore almost always certain. Only in rare cases does a planet change sign on the day of birth itself.

### The aspects

Aspects are particular angles between two planets, for example 90 or 120 degrees. In interpretation they describe how the themes of two planets work together. Because an aspect is rarely exact, astrology works with an orb. The orb is the margin within which an angle still counts as an aspect, typically a few degrees either side of the exact value. Since most planets barely move in a day, the aspects between them stay largely the same. The aspects that are uncertain are mainly those involving the Moon.

## The Moon as a special case

The Moon moves faster than anything else in the chart, covering about 12 to 15 degrees a day. With 30 degrees per sign, this means the Moon changes sign roughly every two to three days. If your birthday falls on such a day, it is unclear without the time which of the two signs it was in.

On other days the Moon sign is clear even without the time. Its exact degree remains imprecise, however. This mainly affects the Moon's aspects, which may or may not form depending on the time.

## What cannot be determined without a birth time

### The ascendant

The ascendant, also called the rising sign, is the zodiac sign rising on the eastern horizon at the moment and place of birth. Because the Earth rotates, the whole zodiac passes over the horizon in the course of a day. The ascendant therefore changes sign about every two hours on average. Without the time, any of the twelve signs is possible. The article [how to calculate your ascendant](/en/guide/ascendant) explains exactly how the calculation works.

### The Midheaven

The Midheaven, abbreviated MC from the Latin "Medium Coeli", is the point of the zodiac that stood highest above the horizon at birth. In interpretation it stands for career and public role. It, too, moves with the Earth's rotation and cannot be determined without the time.

### The houses

Houses are twelve sections of the birth chart, each standing for an area of life such as relationships, home or friendships. The first house begins at the ascendant. Without the ascendant, the houses cannot be fixed. This also leaves open which area of life a planet falls in.

## An approximate time is better than none

Sometimes the time is not known exactly, but roughly. "Just after breakfast" or "in the middle of the night" already narrows things down. With a window of one to two hours, often only one or two ascendants are possible. The houses remain uncertain even then, because a few minutes can decide where the houses begin.

What matters is being honest about the uncertainty. An estimated time presented as exact produces a birth chart built on the wrong foundations.

## Where you might still find your birth time

Before you settle for a horoscope without a time, it is worth checking these sources:

- **Registry office of your place of birth:** The time of birth is usually recorded by the registry office where the birth was registered. In Germany this is the Standesamt, which keeps the birth register. If the time is not on your birth certificate, you can request a full extract from the register from the same office.
- **Parents or relatives:** The time has often stayed in someone's memory or been written down somewhere.
- **Hospital records:** The birth notes from the hospital or maternity unit usually record the time of birth.
- **Family records:** Some families keep certificates and documents that include the time.

You later enter the time as it was locally at the time. The conversion to Universal Time, including summer time, is done using the official time zone database. You do not need to convert anything yourself.
